About us

Welcome to LMDV Capital, an Italian single-family office headquartered in Milan.

Fully backed by Leonardo Maria Del Vecchio, shareholder of EssilorLuxottica Group and Founder and President of Fondazione LMDV, LMDV Capital specializes in investing across multiple asset classes. Our portfolio includes indirect investments in venture and growth capital, private equity and hedge funds, and direct investments in leading Italian companies that embody the excellence of Made in Italy. We are committed to supporting entrepreneurial initiatives that preserve and enhance the authentic spirit of Italian craftsmanship. Our geographic focus spans Europe and North America.

Unlike traditional family offices, we stand out for our strategic approach. As prominent private investors, we prioritize internationally recognized brands in the technology, lifestyle and consumer goods sectors. Leveraging our extensive industry expertise and global network, we identify and unlock growth opportunities, driving long-term success.

Our commitment extends beyond financial returns.

We view entrepreneurs as catalysts for positive, transformative change in both society and the economy. That's why every aspect of LMDV, from our culture to our decision-making processes, is designed to support them on their journey to success.

We believe in a hands-on, long-term approach, working closely with entrepreneurs to achieve impactful results while ensuring sustainable growth. We provide strategic guidance to help them scale responsibly and make a meaningful contribution to a better future.

Join us at LMDV Capital, where we're more than just investors. We're partners in your journey to success.

Our vision

We are committed to enhancing the value of Made in Italy by supporting and relaunching brands that embody the quality, tradition and innovation of our country, with a particular focus on hospitality, entertainment, food and new technologies.

Our goal is to create an ecosystem of resilient and continuously growing businesses that combine the best of Italian tradition with international innovation, so that future generations can recognize our heritage as a symbol of success and quality.

LMDV Capital aspires to become a driver in the entrepreneurial world, where passion and dedication are always directed toward sustainable, responsible and meaningful growth.

Our mission

LMDV Capital's mission is to invest with a clear vision, bringing resources and expertise to entrepreneurs who share our values of excellence, creativity and responsibility.

With a strong focus on Made in Italy, we are dedicated to supporting brands and initiatives across diverse sectors, from hospitality to fine dining, from cinema to fashion, to preventive medicine and artificial intelligence, always maintaining a sustainable approach.

We invest with precision and direct involvement, fostering growth through a long-term vision, flexible governance and a strong commitment to social and economic well-being.

With deep roots in tradition and an innovative outlook, we aim to be the partner that helps businesses succeed, creating synergies among our portfolio companies and generating a positive impact on the global market.

Our areas of interest

As thematic investors, we draw inspiration from innovative entrepreneurs shaping the future of technology across various sectors, including lifestyle. Our thesis is rooted in the belief that significant transformations are imminent, with new realms of innovation emerging periodically.

Here are some of the current focus areas guiding our investments:

  • Collaborative empowerment

    Greatness happens when diverse perspectives come together. By setting egos aside, we work towards a shared goal: helping you build a world-changing business.

  • Integrity and accountability

    We make decisions with integrity, fostering open discussions and embracing diverse viewpoints. Our goal? To be the investor you're proud to have on your cap table.

  • True partnership

    We build genuine, long-term relationships, understanding the evolving nature of venture investments. Our aim? To consistently earn positive recommendations from the founders we've supported.

  • Honesty and transparency

    We prioritize authenticity and openness. We acknowledge mistakes, learn from them, and work collaboratively towards shared goals. You'll always have someone to talk to, day or night.

  • Calculated risk-taking

    We take bold yet well-informed risks, remaining committed to continuous learning. Expect us to challenge you to think big and focus on long-term impact.
